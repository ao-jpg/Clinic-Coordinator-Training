Quick Reference

The whole role at a glance. Print it or keep it at the coordinator station.

◆
Core rule: before the coordinator leaves the patient-flow position, another available team member explicitly accepts the role. There is always one clear answer to "Who is Clinic Coordinator right now?"

Coverage order

  1. Free clinical team member
  2. Free Lab Coordinator or lab team member
  3. Free sterilization technician
  4. Free front desk team member
  5. Free Office Manager

The key word is available. Do not create a new gap to fill this one.

Handoff, four steps

  1. Identify the highest available person
  2. State the transfer directly
  3. Give a brief status
  4. Get their acknowledgment before you leave

Hand back just as clearly when the regular coordinator returns.

Acting coordinator cycle

Look ahead›Choose location›Read note›Prepare›Seat›Yellow: Chair Occupied›Start note›Ask + document›Comfort›Return to open-bay computer

Light bar priority

Worst consequence, first seen.

  1. Bondings and broken brackets
  2. Consults
  3. All other standard appointments
  4. Quick checks

Large-block work (gingivectomy, TAD, direct bond, multiple broken brackets) can be saved for last on purpose.

Chair rules

  • Rooms: adults, long child appointments, TCL, special needs
  • Chair 6: do not default just because it is open
  • Chair 7: absolute overflow

Patient intake

  • Ask about loose, broken, poking, or painful issues
  • Document what matters in the new visit note
  • Communicate leave-by times early
  • Offer TV and make the patient comfortable
  • Return to the computer and ask: "Who is next?"

Five pillars of a complete note

  • Treatment card entry
  • Treatment notes
  • Tooth chart
  • Next-visit note (add NV2, NV3 where you can)
  • GPS note (weeks, minutes, type, special note)

Four out of five is incomplete. You review, the tech authors.

End of day: the two reports

  • Report 1: Missing Entries. Resolve every gap.
  • Report 2: All Appointment Entries with Next-Visit Note.
  • Doctor signs off on both. Print both to PDF and save to the shared folder.
  • Confirm the lab list is current with the full note on every case.
